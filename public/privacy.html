Privacy Policy

Effective: June 6, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how Syntra AI ("Syntra," "we," "us," or "our"), operated by Eris Dothard, collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our services, applications, and integrations.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Contact information: name, email address when you reach out or use our services
  • Account credentials: authentication tokens when you connect third-party platforms
  • Service data: content and information you provide through our automation tools and integrations

Information from Third-Party Platforms

When you authorize integrations with platforms such as TikTok or other social media services, we may receive:

  • Basic profile information (username, display name, profile picture)
  • Content you authorize us to access or manage
  • Analytics and engagement data related to your authorized content

We only access data within the scope of permissions you explicitly grant.

Automatically Collected Information

  • Device and browser information
  • IP address and approximate location
  • Usage patterns and interaction data within our services

2. How We Use Your Information

Purpose Data Used
Provide and operate our Services Account data, service data, third-party tokens
Process third-party integrations OAuth tokens, authorized platform data
Communicate with you Contact information
Improve our Services Usage data, analytics
Comply with legal obligations As required by law

3. Third-Party Platform Data

When you connect third-party platforms (e.g., TikTok) to our Services:

  • We access only the data and permissions you explicitly authorize
  • We do not sell, share, or transfer your third-party platform data to other parties
  • We use third-party data solely to provide the features and automation you requested
  • You can revoke access at any time through the third-party platform's settings or by contacting us
  • Upon revocation, we delete your third-party platform data within 30 days

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share data only in these circumstances:

  • Service providers: trusted tools and infrastructure necessary to deliver our Services (e.g., hosting, analytics)
  • Legal requirements: when required by law, regulation, or legal process
  • Business transfers: in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets
  • With your consent: when you explicitly authorize sharing

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data, including:

  • Encrypted data transmission (TLS/HTTPS)
  • Secure storage of authentication tokens and credentials
  • Access controls limiting data access to authorized personnel
  • Regular review of security practices

No system is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security but commit to promptly addressing any breach.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary to provide our Services or as required by law. When data is no longer needed:

  • Account data is deleted within 30 days of account closure
  • Third-party platform data is deleted within 30 days of access revocation
  • Usage logs are retained for up to 12 months for security and improvement purposes

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Delete your data (subject to legal retention requirements)
  • Revoke third-party platform authorizations at any time
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Object to certain data processing activities

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at agent@syntraai.tech.

8. Children's Privacy

Our Services are not directed to individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors. If we become aware that we have collected data from a minor, we will delete it promptly.

9. Cookies and Tracking

Our website may use minimal, functional cookies to maintain session state and improve performance. We do not use third-party advertising cookies or cross-site tracking.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Material changes will be communicated through our website. The "Effective" date at the top reflects the latest revision.
