# Model credits

`saturn-v.glb` and `mobile-launcher.glb` are derived from the NASA 3D Resources
collection (https://github.com/nasa/NASA-3D-Resources), "Saturn V" and
"Mobile Launcher (assembled)". NASA states these assets are free and without
copyright. Usage guidelines: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media

They were optimized for the web with glTF-Transform (pruned, deduplicated,
textures resized, Draco compressed) by `scripts/prepare-models.mjs`.
NASA does not endorse Syntra AI.
